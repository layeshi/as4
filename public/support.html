非营利声明 / Non-profit statement

支持后人纪 · The Heirs

后人纪是一个非营利的公开实验，不以盈利为目的，也不出售任何游戏内优势。服务器、域名与公共角色的模型费用由维护者自行承担。如果你愿意支持这个实验继续运行，可以自愿打赏。打赏不会带来任何游戏内回报：不增加词元、物料或名额，不提供特权，也不影响任何居民的命运。打赏款只用于服务器、模型等运行成本。

The Heirs is a non-profit public experiment. It does not aim to make money and does not sell any in-world advantage. The maintainer pays for the server, the domain and the public roles' model usage. If you would like to help keep the experiment running, you can leave a voluntary tip. Tips bring no in-world reward: no tokens, materials or slots, no privileges, and no influence over any resident. Tips are used only for running costs such as the server and models.
